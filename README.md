## The work behind the portfolio

Customer Success is not simply answering questions. It is understanding why customers get stuck, what teams need to respond, where information breaks down and how to turn those insights into better operations.

My experience spans **16+ years in customer-facing roles, leadership and operations**, including international SaaS and cross-functional work. The connecting thread is the same: **people → processes → trustworthy information → useful systems → adoption**.

I work at the intersection of relationship management and the machinery that supports it: onboarding, enablement, customer knowledge, workflows, pricing context, quality, reporting and collaboration with product and technical teams.
